The National Science Foundation (NSF) has completed the review and resolution of the Office of
Inspector General (OIG) audit report on costs incurred under NSF awards by the University of Texas at
Austin (UT Austin) during the period of December 1, 2013 through November 30, 2016.

The audit report questioned a total of $283,613 in costs claimed under NSF awards and recommended
that UT Austin strengthen its administrative and management controls for the areas in which findings
were identified. Based on UT Austin’s agreement with the findings and questioned costs identified in
the audit report, NSF’s management decision is to disallow all questioned costs in the amount of
$283,613. The table below summarizes the disallowed costs by funding number:

       Finding                   Description                  Questioned   Allowed         Disallowed
          1        Unreasonable Transactions                     $63,174              $0       $63,174
           2       Purchases Near the Award Expiration           $58,556              $0       $58,556
           3       Unallocable Costs                             $58,209              $0      $58,209
           4       Inadequate Documentation                      $42,947              $0      $42,947
           5       Unallowable Indirect Costs                    $25,767              $0      $25,767
           6       Unreasonable Travel                           $15,604              $0      $15,604
           7       Unallowable Moving and Relocation Costs       $10,505              $0      $10,505
           8       Unallocable Visa Costs                         $6,947              $0       $6,947
           9       Unallowable Promotional Items                  $1,139              $0       $1,139
          10       Underspent Participation Support                 $765              $0         $765
                                                      Total     $283,613              $0     $283,613


UT Austin has provided NSF evidence of $7,053 in repayment. Repayment of the remaining $276,560
may be made via www.pay.gov or check payable to the National Science Foundation. Please
reference OIG Audit Report No. 19-1-012. Any necessary adjustments to ACM$ because of the
repayment will be made by the NSF Division of Financial Management.

Please be aware that UT Austin’s right to appeal cost disallowances by NSF is outlined in Chapter XII.B of
the NSF Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide (PAPPG), which may be found online at:
https://www.nsf.gov/publications/pub_summ.jsp?ods_key=pappg.

NSF accepts UT Austin’s stated corrective actions as responsive to the compliance and internal control
recommendations related to the audit findings and considers all audit report findings and questioned
costs resolved. Upon verification that repayment of all disallowed costs has been completed, the audit
report will be fully closed out by NSF.

UT Austin’s timely responses and full cooperation with the NSF audit resolution process have been
greatly appreciated. Should you have any questions regarding this letter or the NSF audit resolution
process, please contact me at 703-292-4579.
