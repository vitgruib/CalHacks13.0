# 14. Labs submit far more applications just to survive

**Status:** new (2 independent sources)
**One-liner:** With award rates collapsing, PIs are writing many more
applications with little to show for it, so time spent finding the *right*
funder matters more.

## Quotes
- "I submitted 8 grant apps last year and will be 8 this year... Nothing has been awarded, but I don't know what else to do." — r/NIH, 2026-08-29 — [agent-reported] https://www.reddit.com/r/NIH/comments/1w184g9/comment/p6j3tpr/
- "Researchers write proposals and get rejections. It's discouraging for young scientists." — Mark Raizen, UT Austin, Physics Today, 2026-02-10 — [agent-reported] https://physicstoday.aip.org/news/philanthropies-selectively-mitigate-damage-from-lost-federal-science-funding

## What our product does about it
Better-targeted funder matching (04) so fewer, stronger applications go to
funders with a real track record in the lab's area.
