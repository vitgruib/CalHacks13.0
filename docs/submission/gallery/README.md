# Screenshot gallery

20 screenshots of the live app (calhacks130.vercel.app, demo world, simulated today 2026-06-15), taken 2026-10-25 for the Devpost gallery. Captions below.

- `01-landing.jpg` **Landing page.** A 3D lab scene: researchers enter through the research lab, the grant manager through the grant manager network.
- `02-problem-and-solution.jpg` **Problem and solution.** Researchers spend 44% of their federally funded time on administration. One copilot covers planning, money and approvals.
- `03-funds-overview.jpg` **Funds.** Each grant’s real status and money. Notices show the court restoration, then the payment that reopened spending.
- `04-funds-tracker-forecast.jpg` **Grants tracker and forecast.** Spent, committed and truly available money, and where the balance lands at the grant’s end date at the current pace.
- `05-change-grant-status.jpg` **Change a grant’s status.** Active, reinstated by a court with no money yet, or canceled. A reason is required and goes in the log.
- `06-grant-detail.jpg` **Grant detail page.** Award, spent, committed and available at a glance for the Alzheimer’s imaging grant (R01GM061721).
- `07-spending-and-sources.jpg` **Spending and sources.** Payroll and receipts line by line. NIH RePORTER, USAspending and the cancellation list disagree, so the grant is flagged.
- `08-requests-flagged.jpg` **Flagged purchase request.** An espresso machine fails "Category allowed". Every rule shows pass or fail, with the real audit finding behind it.
- `09-requests-bahamas-trip.jpg` **A $9,500 trip to the Bahamas.** Over the $5,000 card limit, and over it again when counted with the same person’s same-day purchases.
- `10-requests-card-limit.jpg` **Card limit hold.** A $6,850 peristaltic pump waits for the grant manager. Approve and Reject unlock only once a reason is typed.
- `11-invoices-waiting.jpg` **Invoices waiting for approval.** One invoice is $800 over its approved purchase order. Another has no purchase order for that vendor.
- `12-invoices-rejected.jpg` **Rejected invoices.** Each rejection says why: coffee isn’t an allowable direct cost, or the delivery address doesn’t match.
- `13-rules.jpg` **Rules.** Every check a purchase request goes through, what passes, and what happens when it fails.
- `14-evals.jpg` **Evals on real NIH grants.** The fund tracker caught 38 of 38 cases where spending should stop or be flagged, and passed 30 of 35 where it should stay open.
- `15-switch-user.jpg` **Switch user.** One click moves between the grant office and each researcher’s own portal.
- `16-researcher-my-grants.jpg` **My grants.** Dr. Priya Nair’s grants in plain words: how much is left and how long it lasts.
- `17-researcher-ask.jpg` **Ask to buy something.** Type what you need and roughly what it costs, then check which grant can pay. Quick questions sit underneath.
- `18-researcher-rule-results.jpg` **Why a request is waiting.** The researcher sees the same rule results as the grant office, and that the grant manager will decide.
- `19-researcher-invoices.jpg` **Researcher invoices.** Approved purchases ready to invoice, then tracked until they’re paid.
- `20-mobile-my-grants.jpg` **On a phone.** The researcher portal at phone width.
